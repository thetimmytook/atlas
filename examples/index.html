Factory ground floor. Drag to pan; use the wheel or pinch to zoom. Drag the frame's bottom-right corner to resize it.

Click or tap a route, point, or independent line to inspect it. A route point shows its own ID and its route. Objects are illustrative positions, not verified loot locations or paths. Use “Move line end” to update the independent line or “Move route point” to update the route. “Add route point” appends a new vertex that can be inspected and moved. “Insert route point” adds a vertex between the first two; “Remove inserted point” removes the most recent insertion. “Replace route interior” replaces every point between the endpoints with two new vertices in one operation, keeping both endpoints. “Add point” creates an independent point at the camera center; “Move added point” moves the most recent one. “Remove added point” detaches that point from the map. It can still be moved while detached; “Restore added point” returns the same point at its current position.
